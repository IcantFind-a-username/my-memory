# Privacy · 隐私说明

**中文**

- 你的记忆只保存在你自己的设备上：桌面版在 `~/PersonalMemory/memory.json`，网页胶囊版在这个浏览器的本地存储里。本项目没有服务器、没有账号、没有统计、没有广告。
- 只有在你明确说“记住”或同意 AI 的保存建议时，才会写入。
- 每次 AI 最多只借走和当前问题相关的一小部分（默认不超过约 300 token）。没有“把全部记忆给 AI”的功能。
- 标为“只给我自己看”的记忆，任何 AI 都看不到内容；标为“敏感”的，默认也不分享。
- 你借给 AI 的内容会发送给你正在使用的 AI 服务商（例如 Anthropic、月之暗面、阿里等），并受它们的隐私政策约束。这一部分不在本项目的控制范围内，所以我们只借最少的内容，并在每次借出时告诉你借了什么。
- 每次读取都会在本地访问记录中登记（时间、条目编号、数量），不记录内容。
- 你可以随时删除任何一条、导出全部、或直接删除整个文件夹。

**English**

- Your memory stays on your own device: `~/PersonalMemory/memory.json` for the desktop extension, this browser's local storage for the capsule page. No server, no account, no analytics, no ads.
- Nothing is saved unless you ask, or agree to the AI's suggestion.
- Each reply may borrow only a small slice relevant to the current message (about 300 tokens by default). There is no "give the AI everything" function.
- Notes marked "only for me" are never shown to any AI; notes marked "sensitive" are not shared by default.
- What is lent to an AI is sent to the AI provider you are using (Anthropic, Moonshot, Alibaba, and so on) under their privacy policy. That part is outside this project's control, which is why we lend as little as possible and tell you what was lent each time.
- Every read is recorded in a local access log (time, note ids, counts), never the content.
- You can delete any note, export everything, or delete the whole folder at any time.
